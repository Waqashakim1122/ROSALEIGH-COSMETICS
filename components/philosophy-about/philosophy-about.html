What We Believe

A different starting point

  • Traceable sourcing, not blended origin
  • Nothing in the jar we can't explain
  • Verified before it's ever released

Most skincare starts in a lab and works backward — finding ingredients that test well, then building a marketing story around them. We started the other way around: with a single ingredient that already worked, structurally close to what human skin produces on its own, and built formulas around it with nothing extra.

If we can't explain why an ingredient is in a jar, it isn't in the jar. No fragrance blends we can't name. No filler oils added just to pad a formula.
