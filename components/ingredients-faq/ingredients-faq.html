Common Questions

About Our Ingredients

Will tallow clog my pores?

No — tallow's fatty acid profile closely mirrors human sebum, so skin recognizes it and absorbs it rather than trapping it on the surface. Every Rosaleigh formula is tested for comedogenicity before it's finished.

Does it smell like beef?

No. Raw tallow has a very mild scent that fades almost entirely once it's rendered and whipped. Our unscented line has no added fragrance; our scented line layers in real botanicals, never synthetic "fragrance" blends.

Is Rosaleigh vegan?

No — grass-fed tallow is an animal-derived ingredient, so our formulas aren't vegan. We chose it deliberately: its structure is closer to human skin than any plant oil we tested, which is why we use it instead of a plant-based alternative.

How is it preserved without parabens?

Tallow is naturally shelf-stable due to its fatty acid structure and low water content, which limits microbial growth on its own. We pair this with vitamin E as a natural antioxidant to protect freshness — no synthetic preservatives needed.

Should I patch test before using it?

We recommend it for any new skincare product, tallow included. Apply a small amount to your inner forearm, wait 24 hours, and check for irritation before applying to your face — especially if you have a known animal-product allergy.
