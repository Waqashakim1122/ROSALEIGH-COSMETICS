Our Journey

From Kitchen Counter to Community

2021

The First Batch

Rosaleigh began as a handmade balm mixed in a kitchen, made to soothe our founder's own reactive skin after years of conventional products failing her.

2022

Small Batch, Big Response

Word spread through local markets. Within a year, the Tallow & Honey Balm sold out at every pop-up, and a waitlist was born.

2023

Partnering with Regenerative Farms

We formed direct partnerships with regenerative, grass-fed farms to source the cleanest tallow available — with full traceability.

2025

100,000+ Customers

Rosaleigh formulas are now trusted by over 100,000 customers, with a 4.9-star average rating across independently verified reviews.
