The Person Behind It

I didn't set out to build a skincare brand.

I set out to fix my own skin after nothing else worked — and refused to put anything in a jar I couldn't explain to my own daughter.

Rosaleigh started on a kitchen counter in 2021, rendering tallow by hand because nothing on the shelf met the standard I wanted for my own family. That same standard is in every jar we ship today.
