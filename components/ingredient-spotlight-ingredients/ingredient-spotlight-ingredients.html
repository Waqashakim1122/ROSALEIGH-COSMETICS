Ingredient Spotlight

Grass-Fed Tallow

A bioidentical match to human skin, rich in fat-soluble vitamins and fatty acids that work in complete harmony with your skin's natural barrier. Here's exactly how it gets from pasture to jar.

01

Origin

Farm

Every batch starts on regenerative pasture, with herds raised on open grass, never grain-finished and never confined. This is what "grass-fed" actually means before it's a label — animals living the way they're built to, on land that's tended rather than depleted.

02

Process

Rendered

The fat is rendered low and slow, never rushed with high heat. This is the step most producers skip past, and it's the one that matters most: gentle heat is what keeps the fat-soluble vitamins A, D, E and K intact instead of cooking them out.

03

Craft

Poured

Still warm, it's hand-poured in small batches — not piped through a line built for volume. Small batches mean we can hold every step to the same standard, instead of scaling past the point where quality is easy to track.

04

Result

Whipped Balm

Cooled and whipped by hand into a soft, spreadable texture — this is what actually reaches your jar. No fillers, no synthetic emulsifiers, nothing added to stretch the batch. Just tallow, whipped until it melts into skin on contact.
