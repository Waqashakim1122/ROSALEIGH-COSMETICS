Frequently Asked

Common Questions

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 1–2 business days and typically arrive within 3–5 business days in the US. Free shipping on orders over $75.

What is your return policy?

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days of purchase for a full refund. Reach out to hello@rosaleigh.com to start a return.

Are your products safe for sensitive skin?

Yes — every formula is dermatologist-inspired and free from parabens, sulfates, phthalates and synthetic fragrance. We recommend a patch test for new products.

Do you offer wholesale or bulk pricing?

We do! Select "Wholesale" in the contact form above and our team will follow up with pricing and minimums.
