Our Story

Skincare, As Nature Intended

Handcrafted in small batches. Rooted in ancestral wisdom.

Meet the Founder

"I created Rosaleigh because I believed skincare should work with your skin's biology — not fight against it."

After struggling with sensitive, reactive skin for years and finding no lasting solutions in conventional skincare, I turned to ancestral wisdom — and the science behind it completely changed my life. Every formula is handcrafted in small batches, using ingredients I'd trust on my own skin and my children's. Nothing more, nothing less.

— Rosaleigh Founder

Our Journey

From Kitchen Counter to Community

2021

The First Batch

Rosaleigh began as a handmade balm mixed in a kitchen, made to soothe our founder's own reactive skin after years of conventional products failing her.

2022

Small Batch, Big Response

Word spread through local markets. Within a year, the Tallow & Honey Balm sold out at every pop-up, and a waitlist was born.

2023

Partnering with Regenerative Farms

We formed direct partnerships with regenerative, grass-fed farms to source the cleanest tallow available — with full traceability.

2025

100,000+ Customers

Rosaleigh formulas are now trusted by over 100,000 customers, with a 4.9-star average rating across independently verified reviews.
